Privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026 · Provided by the Splitzee team

Summary

Splitzee keeps your information on your phone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and we don’t sell or share your personal information. Bill photos are read on your phone. A photo only leaves your phone if your phone can’t read it and our backup scan service is switched on, and then only to read the items.

This policy applies wherever you use Splitzee. It is written to meet the privacy laws of the places Splitzee is available, including the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California’s CCPA/CPRA and other US state laws, Canada’s PIPEDA, Australia’s Privacy Act 1988, Brazil’s LGPD, India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, and New Zealand’s Privacy Act 2020 and Health Information Privacy Code 2020.

Who we are

Splitzee is provided by the Splitzee team. For the purposes of the GDPR and similar laws, we are the “controller” of any personal information we process. Because almost everything stays on your device, in most cases we never receive your information at all.

Information kept on your phone

Your name, surname and phone number; your emergency contact, next of kin and medical insurance details, if you add them.

Your bill history, used for Your Stats and Wrapped: the date and time, the place name, the bill total and currency, what you paid and tipped, how many people were there, the names you gave your friends, what each friend had and paid, and whether they have paid you back.

Your saved groups, your payment details (such as a bank account number, IBAN, UPI ID or payment app handle) if you add them, and your language and country settings.

This information is stored only on your phone (personal and emergency details are encrypted). It is never sent to us. Because we don’t receive it, we can’t see, use or share it.

Some of it is information about other people (your friends, emergency contact and next of kin). Please only add people who are happy for you to do so.

Health information

Medical insurance details can be health-related information, which many laws treat as sensitive (for example “special category data” under the GDPR). It is optional, stays encrypted on your phone, and is only shown to you. You can delete it at any time.

Location

If you allow it, Splitzee uses your location: to find which country you are in (to show the right emergency numbers, currency and payment details), and when you use a safety feature (SOS, sending your location, a missed “Get home safe” check-in, or live location sharing).

Your phone’s own map service (Apple or Google) turns your location into a country. Splitzee keeps only the country, not your location. It doesn’t track you in the background or keep a location history. You can turn location off in your phone’s settings at any time; Splitzee then uses your phone’s region setting.

If automatic safety texts are switched on in a future version, your emergency contact’s phone number, the text and your location would pass through our safety server and an SMS provider so the text can be sent for you. We will update this policy before that happens.

Bill photos

Splitzee reads bill photos on your phone using its built-in text recognition. The photo is not uploaded for this.

If the phone can’t find any items and our backup scan service is switched on, the photo is sent securely to our scan server, which passes it to Anthropic’s Claude AI service (in the United States) to read the items. Our scan server does not save your photo. Anthropic processes it under its own terms and privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy). Where the law requires, such transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Please avoid photographing anything other than the bill, such as card numbers or people. Your friends’ names are never sent to the scan server.

Messages, calls and payments

When you send someone their share, a reminder, an SOS or your location, Splitzee opens your phone’s share menu or Messages app (or copies the text) with the message filled in. You choose who it goes to and whether to send it. Splitzee doesn’t send anything itself.

Call buttons open your phone’s dialler. The Pay button only opens Google Wallet or Apple Wallet. Splitzee never sees your card details and no payment passes through the app.

Why we can use your information (legal bases)

Where the GDPR or a similar law applies: we process information on your device to provide the app you asked for (performance of a contract); we use your location, and any health-related details you add, only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in your phone’s settings or by deleting the details; and we use the backup scan service, if switched on, to provide the feature you asked for.

What we don’t do

We don’t require an account, show ads, use analytics or tracking tools, profile you, or make automated decisions about you. We don’t sell or “share” personal information as those words are defined in California law, and we don’t use it for targeted advertising.

How long it’s kept

Information on your phone is kept until you delete it or delete the app. Bill photos sent to the backup scan service are not stored by our server.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and not to be treated differently for using these rights.

Because your information stays on your phone, you can do most of this yourself: see and edit your details on the Info screen, delete your bill history with “Clear all stats”, delete your info with “Delete my info”, and delete everything by deleting the app.

For anything else, or if you’d like us to confirm what (if anything) we hold about you, email us. We’ll reply within the time your local law requires (usually one month).

You can also complain to your local data protection authority, for example a supervisory authority in the EU, the UK Information Commissioner’s Office, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, Brazil’s ANPD, India’s Data Protection Board, or New Zealand’s Office of the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz).

Children

Splitzee is not designed for children under 13, or under the minimum age for consent to online services in your country (up to 16 in some EU countries).

Security

Personal and emergency details are stored encrypted by your phone (iPhone Keychain or Android Keystore). Any connection to our servers uses encryption (HTTPS). Keep your phone locked to protect the information on it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Splitzee uses information, we will update this policy in the app and change the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will tell you in the app.
